# Requested Stock Finish

An explicit stock-ready request includes UVs, materials, checker inspection, and a test render unless the user requests geometry only. It raises the acceptance standard without authorizing publication, unrelated exports, or file overwrites. Do not guarantee compliance with a stock platform.

Verify sensible material IDs and names, no unintended UV overlap or degenerate islands, consistent texel density, and adequate island padding. Inspect a checker on broad surfaces, corners, and sidewalls; packed rectangles or zero overlap alone do not prove low distortion. Keep fabric at a consistent physical scale and wood grain aligned with each member. Verify weave relief and varnish response in a render. Procedural Noise is not evidence of reproducing a specific fabric or wood.

Use runtime-supported renderer/material capabilities and report unsupported behavior honestly. Preserve the one-node Editable Poly cage and live one-iteration TurboSmooth source. Include checker and test-render evidence with the same silhouette/component comparison required for geometry.

When scene files or exports are requested, verify saved output, reopening, and dependencies without replacing an unsaved user scene. Preserve the editable source and prepare suitable texture maps for portable exports, or disclose procedural-material limitations. An export missing its maps is not a complete textured delivery. Packaging and export formats remain governed by the actual request; never automatically publish.
