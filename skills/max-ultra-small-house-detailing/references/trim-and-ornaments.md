# Swept trim and repeated ornament

Separate the continuous architectural section from sculptural repeats such as corbels or ornament bands. A profile-only request does not imply adding decoration. Follow the spline skill for construction and preserved source copies; keep the current project's profile rather than importing a generic classical style.

## Profile and receiving path

Define a section's mounting datum, projection axis, and height axis. Use real curved transitions where the reference has beads or coves and sharp knots for steps. Build the receiving path at the actual installation level with straight corner/line segments or intentional curves. Verify a short working section against the wall before extending it around the room.

Inspect available Sweep properties and the real custom-section node reference. The generic modifier tool's scalar parameters do not represent every plugin reference property; use a bounded reviewed script when required. Do not assume a setter from another MCP exists. Keep the preserved source separate from the working modifier stack and account for every dependent object before changing a shared section.

Choose section/path smoothing to preserve curved profiles and crisp architectural turns. Smoothing the section and adding a Smooth modifier are different operations. Do not add Smooth or TurboSmooth indiscriminately or use them to hide overlaps. Preserve existing approved stacks; verify any changed smoothing visually with the actual supported API.

Route upper trim against walls, headers, and tall cabinetry at its own elevation. Route baseboards from the finished-floor surface and exposed wall bases, terminating at openings, plinths, and fixed furniture as the design requires. Loose furniture need not interrupt wall trim. An upper perimeter is not automatically a usable lower path. Check corners, short returns, end caps, backing contacts, and the evaluated profile extent rather than only the path centerline.

## Prepare one repeat before distribution

Use a suitable existing asset or model the requested motif. External generation is an optional user-selected source, not a required Tripo workflow or a Max Ultra MCP capability. Confirm available access and import support before depending on it; do not initiate purchases or assume export rights. Check imported physical scale and actual axes before rotating or rescaling; geometry may already be Z-up.

Compare the master with the reference for silhouette, relief, and intentional asymmetry. Inspect mounting surfaces, normals, degeneracy, loose shells, and unintended intersections. A vendor's quad label does not prove a usable subdivision cage. Use the character/object skill only when creating an individual subdivision subject under its contract, not to impose one mesh on the whole architectural assembly.

Fit the master between its receiving planes before making copies. Preserve the specified fixed datum during a partial correction: shortening a lower body need not move the cap or scale the entire object. Recheck shape and UVs after fitting.

## Repeat, corners, and attachment

Use instances while shared edits are useful. Align actual mounting direction, tangent, and elevation, not unverified imported pivot axes. Establish pitch and edge clearance from the brief and physical footprint. For a straight run, distribute centers symmetrically at the chosen pitch where possible; reduce count when the required end clearance cannot fit. Check adjacent runs together for corner collisions.

Continuous small bands need coordinated phase and end or miter units; projecting corbels may instead need clear end margins. Do not apply one corner rule to every motif. Verify a sample corner and short return before distributing throughout the scene.

Attach only when requested or needed for the agreed deliverable, preserving transforms, polygon topology, UVs, materials, and intentional gaps. Make the attachment target independent first. After attachment, former room assemblies may no longer follow the original instance master; query actual dependencies before later edits. Verify bounds and visible part counts before removing any explicitly superseded working nodes, and retain useful sources.

Review a full run and corner close-ups in context. Check silhouette, level, end treatment, adjacent openings/furniture, and mapping through [materials and review](materials-and-review.md). Scope the work to the requested details and user-defined review boundaries.
